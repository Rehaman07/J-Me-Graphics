# J’Me Graphics

> A bold, conversion-focused digital growth experience for ambitious startups.

![J’Me Graphics logo](./public/logo.png)

J’Me Graphics is a modern marketing agency website built around the idea that strong growth comes from the balance of three creative and performance disciplines:

**Organic reach · Visual identity · Paid performance**

The experience combines expressive art direction with clear conversion paths, helping visitors understand the agency’s services, explore its process, estimate potential ROI, and request a custom growth audit.

## Design direction

The interface uses a dark, immersive visual language designed to make the brand’s bright accent colors feel energetic and memorable.

- Dark, glassmorphism-inspired surfaces create depth and hierarchy.
- Green, orange, and blue accents represent the three growth pillars.
- Rounded cards, soft glows, and bold type create a confident startup aesthetic.
- Motion is used to guide attention, reinforce interaction, and make the page feel alive.
- Responsive layouts keep the visual system consistent across desktop and mobile.

## Experience highlights

### Hero experience

A high-impact introduction positions J’Me Graphics as a growth partner and directs visitors toward a complimentary audit.

### Three growth pillars

Interactive service tabs showcase:

1. SEO and viral social growth
2. High-converting graphic design
3. Paid campaigns and lead generation

### Interactive ROI calculator

Visitors can adjust marketing inputs and view a live forecast for projected revenue, impressions, conversions, and ROAS.

### Proof and process

Portfolio work, a step-by-step process timeline, client testimonials, and pricing options build clarity and trust throughout the journey.

### Growth audit wizard

A guided three-step modal collects a visitor’s primary goal, budget range, and contact details before confirming the audit request with an animated success state.
